# Step 03: Discovery Interview (`allium` — Elicit)

1. **Entities & Lifecycle**: Discover domain nouns, inline enum states, `transitions`, and state-dependent fields.

3. **Surfaces & Contracts**: Define boundary `facing`, `context`, `exposes`, `provides`, and `contracts:` (`demands`/`fulfils`).
4. **Ambiguities**: Record unresolved business decisions explicitly as `open question "..."`.
