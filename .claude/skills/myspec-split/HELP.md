**What**: Splits large requirements documents or architectural plans into atomic OpenSpec change stubs.
**When**: When an analysis document or brief covers multiple distinct capabilities or domains.
**How**: `/myspec-split <path-to-document>` (Claude Code: `/myspec:split <path-to-document>`).
**Before**: `/guided-reasoning-flow` or `/myspec-init` · **After**: `/myspec-tdd-spec-reasoning` for the first change in openspec/changes/.
