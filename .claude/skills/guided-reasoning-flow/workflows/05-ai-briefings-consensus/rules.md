# Rules: Workflow 05 — AI-to-AI Briefings & Consensus Convergence

1. **Mandatory English EARS & BDD AI-to-AI Protocol**:
   - WHEN conducting AI-to-AI briefings, the Guided Reasoning Orchestrator SHALL load `the ears-syntax-standards skill` and `the bdd-gherkin-specifications skill` to enforce concise English canonical EARS and declarative `GIVEN-WHEN-THEN` syntax.
2. **Intent Contracts & Falsification Criteria**:
   - The Specialist Brief SHALL declare an **Intent Contract** containing Primary Hypothesis, explicit Falsification Criteria, and Boundary Invariants before proposing technical solutions.
3. **Dialectical Protocol & Devil's Advocate Challenge**:
   - WHEN converging on consensus, the Participating Experts SHALL include a **Devil's Advocate** Antithesis challenging assumptions, edge-case vulnerabilities, and cognitive biases.
4. **Scoped Context-Aware Write Delegation**:
   - The Guided Reasoning Orchestrator SHALL write `brief/topic-[id]-[expert]-brief.md` while granting Scoped Context-Aware Write Delegation only to `cyber-security-expert` for security briefs in `brief/`.
5. **KISS-First & Pragmatic SOLID Governance**:
   - The Participating Experts SHALL evaluate proposals against `the kiss-and-solid-principles skill` (Rule of Three `>= 3` before multi-class patterns, `1:1` DI contracts allowed, Non-Simplification Security Floor).
6. **Expert Questions to Activator (Max 3 / Expert / Topic)**:
   - Each Domain Expert SHALL raise at most 3 non-trivial `qa` questions per topic (1 question per turn via `AskUserQuestion` or AI `GIVEN-WHEN-THEN` exchange), logged in `brief/topic-[id]-expert-activator-qa.md`.
7. **Backward Topic Reconciliation (Max 3 Iterations)**:
   - IF analyzing `TOPIC-N` invalidates an earlier `TOPIC-M`, THEN the Guided Reasoning Orchestrator SHALL re-open and reconcile affected briefs across at most 3 bounded iterations.
